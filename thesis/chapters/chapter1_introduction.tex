\section{Research questions}
\label{sec:rqs}

\begin{description}
  \item[RQ1] Can a multimodal lecture-segmentation pipeline produce
    hierarchical (chapter and subtopic) outputs while remaining open-source
    and reproducible?
  \item[RQ2] Does a learned reliability-weighted fusion outperform fixed-weight
    modality fusion when source quality varies?
  \item[RQ3] Does a small open local language model match closed-API
    alternatives for boundary refinement and chapter titling?
  \item[RQ4] Does explicit hierarchical modelling improve segmentation quality
    over independent flat passes?
  \item[RQ5] Are gains over prior baselines statistically significant under
    paired non-parametric testing on a held-out fold?
\end{description}
