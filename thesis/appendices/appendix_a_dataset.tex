\section{Subtopic annotation guidelines}

The following guidelines were given to annotators for the subtopic annotation
pass (after chapter boundaries were fixed from YouTube metadata):

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{What counts as a subtopic boundary.}
    A subtopic boundary occurs when the speaker transitions from one coherent
    pedagogical sub-idea to another within a chapter.
    Indicators: a change in the specific concept being explained
    (e.g., from \emph{definition} to \emph{worked example}), an explicit
    verbal transition (``Now let us look at\ldots'', ``Moving on to\ldots''),
    or a new figure or slide being introduced.

  \item \textbf{Minimum duration.} Each subtopic segment must be $\geq 60$~seconds.

  \item \textbf{Number per chapter.} 2--5 subtopics per chapter.
    If a chapter has fewer than 120~seconds of content, annotate as a single
    subtopic.

  \item \textbf{Title format.} Short noun phrase, maximum 8 words, preferring
    the speaker's own terminology.

  \item \textbf{Uncertainty.}
    When unsure, insert a boundary.
    Disagreements across annotators contribute to IAA measurement and are
    scientifically valuable.
\end{enumerate}
